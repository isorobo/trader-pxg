# 05 — Key Levels (Support/Resistance + Supply/Demand, merged)

> **Editor's note:** the original list had these as two separate strategies. They're the same core idea — price zones where large orders previously clustered — so they're merged here. "Supply/demand zones" are essentially S/R levels drawn as zones with an institutional narrative attached.

## Core Idea
Markets remember prices. Areas where price previously reversed hard (or launched from) tend to attract orders again. Buy at demand/support, sell at supply/resistance — with confirmation, never blindly.

## How to Identify Zones
- Sharp departure: a strong impulsive move away from a level marks it
- Fresh zones (untested since creation) are strongest
- Each retest weakens a zone — after 3+ touches, expect a break, not a bounce
- Higher timeframes (4H, Daily) produce more reliable zones

## Rules (Long — mirror for short)
1. Price returns to a fresh demand/support zone
2. Bullish rejection (wick + strong close) inside the zone
3. Volume confirmation on the rejection
4. Enter after the confirmation candle — never on first touch alone

**Stop loss:** just beyond the far side of the zone.
**Exit:** next opposing zone / resistance.

## Best Conditions
- Range-bound or gently trending markets
- Higher timeframes

## Avoid
- Major news (levels get steamrolled)
- Thin/illiquid markets
- Zones tested many times

## Stats (rough)
- Win rate: 50–65%
- R:R: 1:1.5 to 1:5 depending on zone quality

## Notes for Automation
This is the hardest of the "simple" strategies to automate because zone identification is subjective. Objective proxies that backtest reasonably: prior day/week high-low, pivot points, volume-profile HVNs, or swing highs/lows from fractal detection. If your AI can't define the zone in code, it can't trade this — don't let it hand-wave.
