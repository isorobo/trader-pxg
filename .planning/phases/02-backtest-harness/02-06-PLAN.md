<objective>
Implement the trade ledger (BACK-05, D-11, D-12): parameterized writes to `backtest_runs`/`backtest_trades`, one row per fill event (scale-out tranches get their own rows, per the orchestrator's locked ledger-granularity resolution), a reproducibility guarantee, and BACK-06's per-strategy attribution grouping.

Purpose: every simulated trade must be attributable to a run and a strategy, and identical seed+params+data must produce an identical ledger (D-12) — otherwise nothing downstream (metrics, Strategy Lab comparisons) can be trusted. BACK-06 also requires per-strategy attribution: given a strategy_id, a caller must be able to pull every trade that strategy has ever produced (across runs) and get a correctly-grouped metrics dict. This plan adds that minimal grouping capability. Cross-run attribution REPORTING (dashboards, tournament comparisons, trend charts) stays out of scope — that is Phase 3/7's job; this plan only guarantees the grouping primitive exists and is correct.
Output: `trader/backtest/ledger.py`, green tests including a reproducibility test and a per-strategy-grouping test.
</objective>
